\question[5] $f(x)=x^3$ funksiyaning hosilasini toping.
\begin{choices}
\choice $x^2$
\CorrectChoice $3x^2$
\choice $3x$
\choice $x^4$
\end{choices}
\begin{solution}
Darajali funksiya qoidasi: $(x^n)'=nx^{n-1}$.
\end{solution}
